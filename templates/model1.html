Model 1 · Integer Programming — Minimum Section Allocation

min Σc xc   subject to   cap · xc ≥ (1 + β) Dc,   xc ≥ 1,   xc ∈ ℤ+
① DECISION VARIABLE
what we choose
How many sections to open for each course.
x_c = 0, 1, 2, 3 …
A whole number — you cannot open half a section.
② OBJECTIVE
what we optimise
Open as few sections as possible.
minimise Σ x_c
Fewer sections = less staff, rooms and timetable.
③ TARGET
the rule we must obey
Every student gets a seat in every course.
cap · x_c ≥ D_c
Seats provided must cover the projected demand of each course.
